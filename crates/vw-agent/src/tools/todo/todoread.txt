Use this tool to read the session's current to-do list. Use it proactively and frequently to stay aware of the task list's status. Use it as often as possible, especially in these situations:
- At the beginning of a conversation, to see what is pending.
- Before starting a new task, to prioritize the work.
- When the user asks about previous tasks or plans.
- When you are unsure what to do next.
- After completing a task, to refresh your understanding of the remaining work.
- Every few messages, to ensure you remain on track.

Usage:
- This tool accepts no parameters. Leave its input blank or empty. Do not include a dummy object, placeholder string, or keys such as `input` or `empty`.
- Returns the to-do list with each item's status, priority, and content.
- Use this information to track progress and plan subsequent steps.
- Returns an empty list when there are no to-do items.
